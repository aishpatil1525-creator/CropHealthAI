Brinjal disease guide 🌿

Learn about common symptoms. Similar symptoms can have different causes.

🌱

Healthy plant

Leaves are generally green and firm, with no obvious unusual spots or wilting.

Reference category
🥀

Bacterial wilt

Rapid wilting can occur while leaves remain attached. Confirm the cause before treatment.

Needs expert confirmation
🍃

Leaf spot

Leaves may develop brown or grey spots and yellowing. Several diseases can cause similar marks.

Inspect symptoms
🍆

Phomopsis blight / fruit rot

May affect leaves, stems and fruits, causing lesions or fruit decay.

Inspect plant and fruit
